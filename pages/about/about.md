## About

![Photo of Jake](media/PhotoOfJake.png)


I’m a video game developer and educator, patent agent, and astrophysicist.

During college, as an astrophysicist, I studied planetary magnetic fields and atmospheres. I conducted research with the Laboratory for Atmospheric Physics, Boston University, and the Institute of Arctic and Alpine Research, in undergraduate researcher positions.

Alongside my scientific pursuits, I became a patent agent by independently studying for and passing the United States Patent and Trademark Office’s patent bar exam. Since 2017, I have worked with inventors and legal teams to write and prosecute patent applications in a range of technologies.

Driven by the combination of creativity and technology, I create video games and procedural generation tools. I have launched [Merchant Isle](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2722600/Merchant_Isle/) on Steam with a team of two other game developers. 

When not delving into the world of game development, I can be found hiking, painting, or playing with my little cats Ash and Luna.
